# Interactive cat

`InteractiveMascot` replaces the `page-mascot` component in the hero. Its direction tracking and click reactions are adapted from page-mascot 0.1.0; the original MIT notice is retained in `LICENSE.page-mascot`.

The frame remains 70 × 70 pixels. A pointer-captured fish button sits at the bottom-right, and its moving visual is portaled to `document.body` with fixed positioning. Drop detection uses the release pointer against the frame's current viewport rectangle. Invalid or cancelled drags return the treat immediately. Edge scrolling allows dragging beyond the visible viewport. Enter or Space on the fish feeds the cat without dragging; Escape cancels a focused drag.

A valid drop immediately starts the preloaded eating sheet and hides the fish until the eating and happy reactions finish. Fifteen frames play at 130 ms each, followed by a 650 ms happy reaction. Cursor tracking and boops cannot replace the feeding expression. A 10,000 ms deadline, measured from the accepted drop, locks feeding while the treat stays hidden for the 2,600 ms feeding sequence, then fades from 0% to 100% over the remaining 7,400 ms. Only a blocked drag attempt shows the countdown tooltip, rounded up to whole seconds; hovering or focusing alone does not. Leaving the treat, blurring, pressing Escape, or finishing cooldown dismisses it. Pointer and keyboard feeding share the same deadline guard, and attempts do not extend it. Timers and event listeners are cleaned up on unmount. Reduced motion uses a still satisfied eating pose and skips the boop squash.

## Eating asset

`public/mascots/cat-eating.webp` was generated using the built-in image generation tool, with both existing cat sheets as references. Each generated cell was registered to the visible bounds of the original center-facing cell, then assembled into a lossless 1080 × 1080 transparent WebP. The nine cells are read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Keep the same grid and alignment when replacing it.

Generation prompt:

> Create a production animation sprite sheet for this exact black and white kitten mascot. Both input images are identity/style/alignment references. Output a square transparent image containing exactly 9 frames in a perfectly regular 3 columns by 3 rows grid, read left to right top to bottom. Match the reference sheet's front-facing center cat: identical black fur white central forehead blaze white muzzle/chest pink ears/blush, dark outline, soft shading. Every frame must have identical body/head registration, front-facing orientation and scale, centered in its equal square cell; preserve the reference amount of transparent padding and chest baseline. Animate a subtle eating/chewing sequence: frame 1 eyes happily closed mouth slightly open, 2 mouth closed cheeks puffed, 3 mouth chewing slightly to left, 4 small open chewing mouth, 5 mouth closed cheeks puffed, 6 chewing slightly right, 7 satisfied closed smile, 8 tiny tongue licking lips, 9 delighted satisfied smile. Only mouth cheeks and eyelids change, head/body remain aligned. No fish, paws, food, hearts, particles, text, labels, borders, grid lines or background. True alpha transparency. Intended for a 70px website avatar, so expressions must be clear.
